From reading the configuration
  SCREENOS_ZONE_OBJECT: the address name 'printer' stands for different addresses in the books of zones DMZ, Trust; each is kept as 'printer (zone)'
  ZONE_NETWORKS: the networks behind the zones, from the addresses of their interfaces and the static routes: DMZ=10.20.0.0/24; Lab=10.40.0.0/24; Servers=10.30.0.0/24; Trust=10.10.0.0/16,10.11.0.0/16,2001:db8:10::/64; Untrust=0.0.0.0/0,203.0.113.0/24. Targets and --compare that need the networks of the zones take them from here where --zone-network does not give them
  SCREENOS_PREDEFINED: predefined services are not in the configuration; FWShift took these from its table: FTP-Get, HTTP, HTTPS, PING, SMTP, SSH
  SCREENOS_POLICY_ORDER: 1 policies were placed by 'set policy move', 'top' or 'before'; the rules are in the order that results

Notes
  (other zones): zones the policy does not name. A rule for 'any' zone applies to them as well.
  1 rule(s) never match and are left out; 'fwshift audit' names them.
  A rule the model does not hold exactly (its findings say why) is shown wherever its zones reach, on every service, whatever its action, marked ~: what it really does is not known.
  ~ at most this. Part of the rule could not be read exactly (a host name, a dynamic or unsupported object, a rule the parser reported as approximated), the blocking rules above it could not be followed, or the address a NAT rule translates to cannot be read.
  svc:NAME: a service of the source that could not be read (an application or category of the vendor, a service without ports). The rule allows that, whatever it is.
  The matrix shows what the policy can allow for some pair of addresses of two zones, not that every address may. It is read from the configuration: routing, NAT that fails and what listens on a port are not in it.
