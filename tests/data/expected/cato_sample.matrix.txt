From reading the configuration
  CATO_SOURCE: 4 sites as zones (Brussels-HQ, Antwerp-DC, Ghent-Plant, Leuven-Lab), remote-users and internet; the WAN Firewall (first match, implicit block) decides between sites and remote users, the Internet Firewall (first match, implicit allow, written as a last rule) from them to the internet; traffic inside one network range does not reach Cato and is allowed
  CATO_INTERNET_TOP: a hidden rule of Cato above the Internet Firewall blocks connections Cato deems illegal or malicious; it is not in the answer and not converted
  CATO_INBOUND: traffic from the internet to the sites is decided by Remote Port Forwarding, which is not read: in the model nothing from the internet is allowed
  CATO_REMOVED: rules marked removed in the revision read: left out: Retired rule

Notes
  1 rule(s) never match and are left out; 'fwshift audit' names them.
  A rule the model does not hold exactly (its findings say why) is shown wherever its zones reach, on every service, whatever its action, marked ~: what it really does is not known.
  ~ at most this. Part of the rule could not be read exactly (a host name, a dynamic or unsupported object, a rule the parser reported as approximated), the blocking rules above it could not be followed, or the address a NAT rule translates to cannot be read.
  The matrix shows what the policy can allow for some pair of addresses of two zones, not that every address may. It is read from the configuration: routing, NAT that fails and what listens on a port are not in it.
