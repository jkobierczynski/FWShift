From reading the configuration
  VYOS_FORMAT: read as the configuration of VyOS 1.4 or later
  HOST_CHAIN_NOT_CONVERTED: ipv4 input filter (2 rules) filters traffic of the router itself, not forwarded traffic, and is not converted
  VYOS_EMPTY_GROUP: groups without a member: a rule that names one matches nothing (VyOS warns about them and takes them): address-group EMPTY
  VYOS_FQDN: host names (domain-group, fqdn), which the router resolves while it runs: converted as host name objects (UPDATES)
  VYOS_QUEUE: rules hand packets to a program (action queue), which decides them: ipv4 name VPN-DMZ rule 20
  VYOS_ZONES: the zone-based firewall was read after the forward filter, as a second chain at the forward hook: traffic into a zone from an interface in no zone, or from a zone without a rule-set for its IP version, is dropped; traffic to an interface in no zone is decided by the forward filter alone
  VYOS_LOCAL_ZONE: the local zone (traffic to and from the router itself) is not converted
  FORWARD_POLICY_ACCEPT: VyOS accepts what no rule of the forward filter decides unless its default-action is drop; the model records that as its default action

Notes
  2 rule(s) that allow something are in NO conduit: 'ipv6-zone-WAN.from-LAN.default', 'ipv6-zone-DMZ.from-WAN.WAN-DMZ-6-20'. Their zones and their addresses do not go together with the networks given.
  (other zones): zones the policy does not name. A rule for 'any' zone applies to them as well.
  A rule the model does not hold exactly (its findings say why) is shown wherever its zones reach, on every service, whatever its action, marked ~: what it really does is not known.
  ~ at most this. Part of the rule could not be read exactly (a host name, a dynamic or unsupported object, a rule the parser reported as approximated), the blocking rules above it could not be followed, or the address a NAT rule translates to cannot be read.
  The matrix shows what the policy can allow for some pair of addresses of two zones, not that every address may. It is read from the configuration: routing, NAT that fails and what listens on a port are not in it.
