It stops at its first lines when objects of an earlier conversion are on the router, and at the first line the router refuses (lines before it stay; on RouterOS 6 the /ipv6 lines need the ipv6 package). Nothing changes traffic until the disabled jump rules at its end are enabled: enable them in Safe Mode, and move each to where it belongs in its chain (in forward, after the rules that accept established connections).
