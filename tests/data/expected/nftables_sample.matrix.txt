From reading the configuration
  CHAIN_NOT_CONVERTED: chain prerouting of table inet raw at the prerouting hook (priority -300, policy accept) can drop forwarded traffic as well and is not converted
  HOST_CHAIN_NOT_CONVERTED: chain input of table inet filter (3 rules, policy drop) filters traffic of the host itself, not forwarded traffic, and is not converted
  SEVERAL_BASE_CHAINS: IPv4 traffic passes 2 base chains at the forward hook: inet f2b forward (-5), inet filter forward (0). It is forwarded only if each of them accepts it; they were combined into one list of rules
  DYNAMIC_SET: sets filled at run time decide what their rules match; those rules are not converted: @banned (flags timeout)

Notes
  (other zones): zones the policy does not name. A rule for 'any' zone applies to them as well.
  ~ at most this. Part of the rule could not be read exactly (a host name, a dynamic or unsupported object, a rule the parser reported as approximated), the blocking rules above it could not be followed, or the address a NAT rule translates to cannot be read.
  The matrix shows what the policy can allow for some pair of addresses of two zones, not that every address may. It is read from the configuration: routing, NAT that fails and what listens on a port are not in it.
