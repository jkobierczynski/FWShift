From reading the configuration
  STORMSHIELD_SOURCE: 5 interfaces as zones (out, in, dmz1, lab, labbridge); filter slot 05 after global slot 01: 16 rules, first match; what no rule matches is blocked
  STORMSHIELD_IMPLICIT: the implicit rules (administration, DNS, IPsec, SSL VPN and the other services the firewall answers itself) come before the policy and are not converted
  STORMSHIELD_DELEG: global policies that end in a deleg of everything: the rest goes to the local policy: Global/Filter/01
  STORMSHIELD_INTERNALS: rules with Network_internals or Internet, read as the networks of the protected interfaces and everything else: networks behind them by routes are not known: local 2, local 4, local 6
  STORMSHIELD_PROTOCOL_ANALYSIS: rules that force the analysis of an application protocol: read as not narrowing what the rule matches: local 15
  STORMSHIELD_NAT_NOT_READ: NAT rules of the slot are not read (the filter rules name the addresses as they arrive, so they do not depend on them): 1 lines in Filter/05

Notes
  (other zones): zones the policy does not name. A rule for 'any' zone applies to them as well.
  ~ at most this. Part of the rule could not be read exactly (a host name, a dynamic or unsupported object, a rule the parser reported as approximated), the blocking rules above it could not be followed, or the address a NAT rule translates to cannot be read.
  The matrix shows what the policy can allow for some pair of addresses of two zones, not that every address may. It is read from the configuration: routing, NAT that fails and what listens on a port are not in it.
