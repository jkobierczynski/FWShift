From reading the configuration
  ZSCALER_SOURCE: 4 locations (HQ-Brussels, Branch-Ghent, HQ-Servers, Guest-WiFi), road-warrior (Client Connector outside a location) and internet; the rules decide traffic from the locations to the internet, first match in ascending order, and the default rule decides the rest
  ZSCALER_OUTBOUND_ONLY: ZIA filters traffic to the internet only: traffic between locations, inside one and from the internet does not pass it, and the model blocks it (no rule allows it). A target that sees such traffic needs rules for it
  ZSCALER_FIREWALL_OFF: locations whose firewall is off (ofwEnabled false): the rules do not decide their traffic on ZIA, the model applies them anyway: Guest-WiFi
  ZSCALER_RANK: admin ranks out of the rule order: read in the rule order: Ranked web (rank 3 below rank 7)

Notes
  A rule the model does not hold exactly (its findings say why) is shown wherever its zones reach, on every service, whatever its action, marked ~: what it really does is not known.
  ~ at most this. Part of the rule could not be read exactly (a host name, a dynamic or unsupported object, a rule the parser reported as approximated), the blocking rules above it could not be followed, or the address a NAT rule translates to cannot be read.
  The matrix shows what the policy can allow for some pair of addresses of two zones, not that every address may. It is read from the configuration: routing, NAT that fails and what listens on a port are not in it.
