Interfaces of the router in no zone: VyOS drops their traffic into a zone and leaves traffic from a zone to them to the forward filter; that traffic is decided by chain FWS-NO-ZONE (the rules for any destination and the default of the policy), jumped to from rule 10000 of the forward filter for what leaves by an interface not in interface group FWS-ZONES. A zone of the same name that exists gets these interfaces and chains in addition to its own; a chain set for a pair that has one replaces it.
