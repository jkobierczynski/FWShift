Notes for the whole policy
  [needs-review] ROUTEROS_LOAD: load the script with '/import file-name=FILE verbose=yes'. It stops at its first lines when objects of an earlier conversion are on the router, and at the first line the router refuses (lines before it stay; on RouterOS 6 the /ipv6 lines need the ipv6 package). Nothing changes traffic until the disabled jump rules at its end are enabled: enable them in Safe Mode, and move each to where it belongs in its chain (in forward, after the rules that accept established connections). Chain fwshift-forward ends with an explicit drop, so the rules of forward after the jump are no longer reached by forwarded traffic. To take a conversion away again: /ip firewall filter remove [find where chain~"^fwshift-" or jump-target~"^fwshift-"]; /ipv6 firewall filter remove [find where chain~"^fwshift-" or jump-target~"^fwshift-"]; /ip firewall nat remove [find where chain~"^fwshift-" or jump-target~"^fwshift-"]; /ip firewall address-list remove [find where list~"^fws-"]; /ipv6 firewall address-list remove [find where list~"^fws-"]; /interface list member remove [find where list~"^fws-"]; /interface list remove [find where name~"^fws-"]
