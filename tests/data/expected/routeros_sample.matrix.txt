From reading the configuration
  ROUTEROS_ZONES: the interface lists the rules use do not overlap: each is a zone (DMZ: vlan20-dmz, GUEST: vlan30-guest, LAN: bridge-lan, WAN: ether1); an interface in none of them is a zone of its own
  HOST_CHAIN_NOT_CONVERTED: chain input of /ip firewall filter (2 rules) filters traffic of the router itself, not forwarded traffic, and is not converted
  TABLE_NOT_CONVERTED: /ip firewall mangle has 1 rules and is not converted (it can mark connections and routes, which the filter rules may test)
  ROUTEROS_PASSIVE: 2 rules with action log, passthrough or add-*-to-address-list decide nothing and were left out. A log rule directly before a rule with the same match, or one that matches everything, sets the logging of the rules it logs for
  ROUTEROS_DYNAMIC_LIST: address lists that rules fill while the router runs decide what their rules match; those rules are not converted: /ip scanners
  ROUTEROS_FQDN_LIST: address lists that hold host names, which RouterOS resolves while it runs: converted as host name objects (mirrors)
  ROUTEROS_BRIDGE: traffic between the ports of one bridge (bridge-lan) does not pass the IP firewall of the source (use-ip-firewall=no); the conversion is of routed traffic
  FORWARD_POLICY_ACCEPT: RouterOS accepts what no rule of the forward chain decides; the model records that as its default action

Notes
  (other zones): zones the policy does not name. A rule for 'any' zone applies to them as well.
  1 rule(s) never match and are left out; 'fwshift audit' names them.
  A rule the model does not hold exactly (its findings say why) is shown wherever its zones reach, on every service, whatever its action, marked ~: what it really does is not known.
  ~ at most this. Part of the rule could not be read exactly (a host name, a dynamic or unsupported object, a rule the parser reported as approximated), the blocking rules above it could not be followed, or the address a NAT rule translates to cannot be read.
  The matrix shows what the policy can allow for some pair of addresses of two zones, not that every address may. It is read from the configuration: routing, NAT that fails and what listens on a port are not in it.
