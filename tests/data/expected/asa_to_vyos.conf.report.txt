  [needs-review] VYOS_LOAD: load the commands in configuration mode: 'configure', paste them, 'compare', then 'commit-confirm 10' (unless 'confirm' follows within 10 minutes, VyOS goes back to the configuration before; older releases reboot to do so) and 'save'. A commit applies all of them or none. The policy is written as the zone-based firewall: zones outside, inside, dmz, guest, and a chain FWS-<from>-<to> for each pair of zones that needs one. Interfaces of the router in no zone: VyOS drops their traffic into a zone and leaves traffic from a zone to them to the forward filter; that traffic is decided by chain FWS-NO-ZONE (the rules for any destination and the default of the policy), jumped to from rule 10000 of the forward filter for what leaves by an interface not in interface group FWS-ZONES. Check that the forward filter has no rule 10000 (show firewall ipv4 forward filter), or give another number with --policy-id-start; its rules with lower numbers come first, and those after rule 10000 are no longer reached by what the chain decides, as it ends with the default of the policy. The forward filter of the router still applies before the zones (an accept there goes on to the zones, a drop is final). A zone of the same name that exists gets these interfaces and chains in addition to its own; a chain set for a pair that has one replaces it. NAT rules are numbered from 10000: rules of the router with lower numbers come first. To take a conversion away: delete the groups, chains, zones and NAT rules the commands name (all under FWS-, and the NAT rules from 10000 on). Assumed: names of up to 63 characters
