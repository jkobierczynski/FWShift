Notes
  trust -> untrust: the default ports of application(s) bittorrent are not in the application map (--app-map): what differs on or off them is possible only.
  The users of the rules are compared by name: what a user group, a custom URL category or a schedule holds is not in the model. Where the two define one of the same name differently, connections may differ that are not shown.
  The NAT rules are not compared; the rules are, as the model holds them (for a source that matches after destination NAT, with the addresses before it as well).
  (elsewhere): addresses in none of the networks given, compared as a zone neither policy names; where they lie behind a zone, give them to it.
  Compared from the configurations: what the platforms do around the rules (routing, implicit rules, NAT that fails) is not in it.
