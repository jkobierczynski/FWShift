From reading the configuration
  LANCOM_UF_FORMAT: read 14 desktop connections, 15 desktop objects and 13 services from the answers of the REST API. LANCOM publishes the keys of that API only by the examples of its LMC add-ins (networks, desktop connections, user-defined services); the keys of hosts, IP ranges, groups, Internet objects and of a rule other than those are assumed, and an object, service or rule with a key the parser does not know is not used. The desktop only allows: what no connection allows is blocked
  OBJECTS_MISSING: objects and services the connections name are not in the input, and the rules that name them are not used: '0e5f1a2b-0000-4000-8000-000000000999'. Save the answers of every collection (section 3)
  CONNECTION_KEYS_NOT_READ: desktop connections hold keys the parser does not know: 'nat'. They are kept with the rules as how they handle traffic, and not converted; if one of them narrows what a connection lets through, the converted rules allow more
  VPN_USER_OBJECTS: VPN and user objects are not read: the rules of connections to and from them are not used
  MASQUERADING_READ: masquerading set on services was read as NAT rules to the address of the outgoing interface. A NAT source address other than that, the NAT of a connection and DMZ (port forwarding) are not read

Notes
  (other zones): zones the policy does not name. A rule for 'any' zone applies to them as well.
  A rule the model does not hold exactly (its findings say why) is shown wherever its zones reach, on every service, whatever its action, marked ~: what it really does is not known.
  ~ at most this. Part of the rule could not be read exactly (a host name, a dynamic or unsupported object, a rule the parser reported as approximated), the blocking rules above it could not be followed, or the address a NAT rule translates to cannot be read.
  svc:NAME: a service of the source that could not be read (an application or category of the vendor, a service without ports). The rule allows that, whatever it is.
  The matrix shows what the policy can allow for some pair of addresses of two zones, not that every address may. It is read from the configuration: routing, NAT that fails and what listens on a port are not in it.
